About the Big Box Guy

Big Box Guy is a guy. His thing is boxes. He has been the Big Box Guy for as long as anyone can remember, and he has asked us not to look into it further.

Our story

In the beginning there was a box. Inside the box was a box. Big Box Guy opened it, which is when the trouble started. He has not stopped since, and he has built a company around not stopping. Today, bigboxguy.com is the world's leading provider of boxes that contain boxes, a category we invented, dominate, and are the only entrant in.

We believe every box deserves to be opened. We believe every opened box deserves to contain a box. We believe in very little else, and we are at peace with that.

"People ask me what's in the box. I tell them. They ask again. I tell them again. I have been having this conversation for eleven years."— Big Box Guy, Founder & Chief Box Officer

Mission

To put a box in every box, and a box in that one, until the heat death of the universe or the end of the cardboard, whichever comes first. (It will be the cardboard.)

Values

  • Box. It is the first thing we think about in the morning.
  • Integrity. Every box contains exactly what we say it contains, which is a box.
  • Transparency. Our boxes are not transparent. We are, though, about that.
  • Sustainability. The box you are opening is the box you will open next. Nothing is wasted. Nothing is gained either.
